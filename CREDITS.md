# Credits

Magnistra is an independent, unofficial Instagram client for legacy iOS.

It incorporates open-source components developed and maintained by their respective contributors:

- [Lottie for iOS](https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-ios) — Airbnb, Brandon Withrow and contributors; animation rendering.
- [vodozemac](https://github.com/matrix-org/vodozemac) — Matrix.org contributors; Olm/Megolm encryption.
- [curl](https://curl.se/) and [OpenSSL](https://www.openssl.org/) — secure networking.
- [libsodium](https://libsodium.org/) — cryptographic primitives.
- [Opus](https://opus-codec.org/) and [Ogg](https://xiph.org/ogg/) — audio.
- [libwebp](https://github.com/webmproject/libwebp) — WebP decoding and encoding.
- [JSONKit](https://github.com/johnezang/JSONKit) — JSON processing.
- [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/) and its contributing crate authors — Rust-based cryptographic bridge.
- [Mozilla](https://www.mozilla.org/) — public CA certificate collection.
